Training advice that actually fits your Rottweiler

Your Rottweiler is strong, smart, watchful and devoted. Generic training tips often skip the things Rottweiler owners care about most: a big dog who pulls, guarding instincts, calm around strangers, and a growing body that needs care.

How to Train Your Rottweiler is a reward-based training ebook written around this one breed. It takes you from the first day of training through a 30-day plan, with short daily sessions and printable checklists.

What's inside the book (78-page PDF, 6 × 9 in)

  • Module 1, Why Breed Matters: drive, energy and how Rottweilers learn, plus a profile of your dog.
  • Module 2, Foundations: rewards, markers, timing and short sessions.
  • Module 3, Core Obedience: sit, down, stay, come, leave it and loose-leash walking.
  • Module 4, House & Crate Training: potty training, a crate your dog likes, alone time and house manners.

How we train

Reward-based methods only. No choke, prong, shock or bark collars, no leash jerks, no punishment.

For guarding, aggression, fear or medical concerns, see your veterinarian first, then a certified trainer or veterinary behaviorist. The book shows you how to prepare for those visits.

Who it's for

  • New Rottweiler puppy owners who want to start right
  • Owners of adolescent or adult Rottweilers who pull, jump, bark or struggle to settle
  • Families who want kind, consistent rules everyone can follow

Not for: anyone looking for punishment-based or “dominance” methods, or as a replacement for professional help with serious aggression or guarding.
